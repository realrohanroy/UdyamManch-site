A Journey Rooted in Rajasthan

In 1970, in Rajasthan, a boy named Kishan Panchal was born. At that time, nobody could have known how closely his name would one day connect with his life's work.

Because over the years, Kishan would become deeply connected with the Kisan — किसान. Not just as a bunisessman, but as someone who spent his life understanding agriculture, farmers, technology and the real problems that exist beyond city offices and presentations.

His story did not begin with a big startup. It began in the fields — with farmers, with conversations, and with a simple question: “How can farming become better?”

From One Venture to a Bigger Vision

Before Farming Solutions India, there were other chapters — Seema Enterprises and Green Bio Tech. Each phase taught a new lesson, brought him closer to the farmer and helped him understand the bigger picture. Over time, the journey moved from supplying products to providing complete agricultural solutions.

Expanding Beyond Rajasthan

What started in Rajasthan gradually expanded across India. Today, Farming Solutions India works with farmers and projects in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Assam, Karnataka, Jharkhand, Odisha, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Haryana — each with its own challenges, climate and opportunities.

Rajasthan Maharashtra Gujarat Assam Karnataka Jharkhand Odisha Punjab Uttar Pradesh Chhattisgarh Haryana

The Philosophy: Protected Agriculture & Farmers First

For Kishan Panchal, the mission has never just been about commercial installations; it is about resilience. Unpredictable monsoons, groundwater depletion, and climate volatility mean that modern protected farming techniques — shade nets, polyhouses, high-efficiency micro-irrigation, and solar pumping systems — are essential safeguards for Indian farmers.

By designing affordable, climate-appropriate infrastructure and standing side-by-side with cultivators until their first harvest flourishes, Farming Solutions India represents the true spirit of Indian grassroots entrepreneurship.
